# TABStudio Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-07-25

This page explains how TABStudio (the "App") handles user information.

## 1. Collection of Personal Information

The App **does not collect personal information from users**.

The App does not acquire or store information that could identify individual
users, such as name, address, phone number, or email address. There is no
account registration or login functionality.

## 2. Handling of Score Data

Guitar Pro format (.gp/.gp5, etc.) tablature data that you load, as well as
project files you create and save, are **processed entirely on your own
PC**. This data is never sent to an external server.

## 3. About AI Features

The App has planned AI-based transcription functionality that would
automatically generate tablature from video or audio, but **this feature is
disabled in version 0.1.0**. In the current version, no data related to this
feature is transmitted externally.

## 4. About Qt WebEngine

The App uses Qt WebEngine (a Chromium-based display component) internally to
display tablature. This is used as a rendering engine to draw scores within
the App and is not intended for browsing external websites.

## 5. About Crash Information

If the App terminates unexpectedly, a diagnostic crash dump may be saved
**only on your own PC**. This dump is never automatically sent externally.
When submitting a bug report, you may optionally choose to include its
contents (see "6. Bug Reports and Feature Requests" below).

## 6. Bug Reports and Feature Requests

When you submit a bug report or feature request from the App's Help menu,
the report is posted as a GitHub (external service) Issue through your own
action. The App does not send information automatically; you review, edit,
and submit the content yourself.

Handling of submitted content is governed by
[GitHub's Terms of Service and Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy).

## 7. Sending Information to External Services

Except for the GitHub Issues submission described in Section 6 above (which
occurs only through your own action), the App **does not send personal
information to any external service**.

## 8. Changes to This Policy

If the information handled changes due to changes in the App's
specifications (including future feature additions), this policy will be
updated. When there is an important change, the "Last updated" date on this
page will be updated to notify you.

## 9. Contact

Questions about this policy can be directed to
[GitHub Issues](https://github.com/nagomi97-kaworu/tabstudio-help/issues).
